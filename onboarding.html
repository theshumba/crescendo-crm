Section 05

How to Qualify Leads

Qualification is the foundation of every successful outreach. Do it well and the rest follows.

The 5 Qualification Areas

For every lead, assess these five dimensions in the CRM.

1
Strategy & Design
Do they need brand strategy, positioning, or creative direction?
2
Experience Design
Do they need UX improvement or better digital product experience?
3
Digital Commerce
Do they need e-commerce, conversion optimisation, or digital growth?
4
Growth Intent
Are they expanding, hiring, entering new markets, launching products?
5
Weaknesses
What gaps can we address? Poor website, weak brand, no online ordering?

How to Research a Lead

Six steps to properly qualify every lead before outreach.

1
Visit their website — desktop and mobile. Note design quality, messaging clarity, and user experience.
2
Check their Instagram and social media — brand consistency, engagement rates, content quality.
3
Look at their Google reviews — customer sentiment, recurring complaints, reputation gaps.
4
Note what's working and what's not — be specific. "Homepage loads slowly on mobile" beats "website needs work."
5
Fill in the qualification fields in the CRM — all five areas from the previous slide.
6
Use the "Generate Email" button to create a personalized outreach email based on your qualification data.
Spend 5–10 minutes per lead. Quality research leads to quality conversations. Rushing this step undermines everything that follows.
Section 06

Cold Outreach

Calls, emails, and follow-ups. The playbook for opening doors.

The Cold Call Script

Follow this structure. Keep it natural, not robotic.

1
"Hi, is this [Name]?" — Confirm you're speaking to the decision maker.
2
"My name is [Your Name], I'm calling from Crescendo Strategy in Cambridge."
3
"I was looking at [Business Name] online and noticed [specific observation]." — Use your qualification research here.
4
"We work with businesses like yours on [relevant pillar] and I wanted to share a couple of quick ideas."
5
"Would you be open to a quick 10-minute call this week to walk through what we spotted?"
6
If yes → Book the meeting for a founder. If not now → "No problem, can I send you a short email with the specifics?"
7
Always be friendly, not pushy. You're offering value, not selling.

Email Outreach

Rules for email outreach that gets responses.

  • The CRM has a "Generate Email" button that creates personalized emails based on qualification data
  • Always make observations specific and provable — reference real things you noticed
  • Subject lines should be short and specific to their business — no generic sales language
  • Always offer value first — a mini audit, quick fixes, diagnostics. Not a pitch.
  • Follow up 2 business days later if no response
  • Never send a template you haven't reviewed for accuracy — always read before you send

Email Examples

Two real examples showing the level of specificity we expect.

Restaurant Example
Subject: Quick note about [Restaurant Name]...

We've helped similar restaurants streamline their digital presence and increase online orders by 40%+. Would you be open to a quick 10-minute call this week? I'd love to share a couple of specific ideas.

Voicemail & Gatekeeper Scripts

What to say when you don't reach them directly. Scripts for every common wall.

Leaving a Voicemail

Keep it 15–20 seconds. Long voicemails get deleted.

1
Name + company — "Hi, this is {YourName} from Crescendo Strategy"
2
Why you're calling — "I was looking at {BusinessName} and noticed {one specific thing}"
3
Brief value offer — "I'd love to share a couple of quick ideas that could help"
4
Callback number + your name again
Sample voicemail
"Hi, this is Muneeb from Crescendo Strategy. I was looking at {BusinessName} and noticed something on your booking flow I thought you'd want to know about. Happy to share a quick audit if useful. My number is {number} — that's Muneeb from Crescendo."
Always repeat your name and number at the end. People scribble while listening.

Getting Past the Gatekeeper

When a receptionist or PA picks up, be polite, confident, direct — not apologetic.

Opening

"Hi, is {DecisionMakerName} available? We connected online about {topic} and I wanted to follow up."

If they ask why

"Just following up on something we discussed — {BusinessName}'s website. It's a quick call."

If blocked

"No problem. What's the best way to reach them — email or a specific time?"

Rules

  • Ask for the decision maker by name. Don't launch into your pitch.
  • Never lie. Never say you're a friend or family. Be professional.

If really blocked

  • Send a short email referencing the call attempt, then try again in 3–5 days
  • Try different times — early AM, after 5pm, Friday afternoon

Your Pitch, Three Lengths

30 seconds, 60 seconds, 2 minutes. Know all three cold.

30-Second / 60-Second / 2-Minute

Match the pitch to the moment. Never overshoot the interest level.

30-second — elevator or first 30s of a cold call
"We're Crescendo Strategy — we help ambitious businesses earn the trust of the next generation. We do strategy, brand, and digital commerce — but we don't just advise, we build. Most of our clients are founder-led and stuck between a great product and slow growth."
60-second — phone call after interest
"We work with businesses that have built something good and know there's more. Our three pillars are growth strategy, brand and experience design, and digital commerce. So we'll help you figure out where growth comes from, then we'll design the brand and digital systems to get you there. We're based in Cambridge, founder-led team of three, plus consultants. Projects typically run 2–4 months, then we stay on as a retainer partner. Minimum engagement is £15K but most clients do more — because we tie the work to revenue outcomes."
2-minute — deeper conversation
"The short version: we're a consultancy that doesn't just produce decks. We do the strategy, build the brand, design the experience, and ship the digital systems — so by the end you have working infrastructure, not a PDF. We work with founder-led businesses that are past product-market fit but stuck on growth. Usually that means good reviews but weak website, strong Instagram but no retention, great in-person experience but no way to scale beyond location. We come in, run a foundation project — 2 to 4 months, £15K to £20K plus — where we do the audit, build the brand, fix the website, wire up the measurement, and hand you a working growth engine. Then we stay on as a retainer to keep optimising. The team's Melusi for creative direction, Josh for strategy and commercial, Ayoub for content performance. We're founder-led every engagement — no handing off to juniors. We're in Cambridge but work UK-wide."

Handing Off Meetings to the Founders

When you book a discovery call for Melusi, Josh, or Ayoub — here's the workflow.

1
Click "Generate Brief" on the lead in the CRM
2
Review the auto-generated briefing doc
3
Add context the CRM doesn't have — tone of voice, main concern, specific questions they asked
4
Send to the founder at least 24 hours before the meeting
5
Include: meeting date/time, lead name, angle to take, opening questions, red flags
6
The founder walks in prepared — no cold reads, no "who am I meeting again"
Rule: No meeting happens without a brief. Even a 3-line brief is better than nothing.
